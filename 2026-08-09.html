MMarkets and Economy

  • The Fed held its policy rate at 3.50%-3.75%, but Governor Cook signaled readiness to hike further as core inflation stays above target, and markets now price 1-2 hikes by year end instead of the cuts they expected earlier this year.
  • Unemployment held at 4.2% with solid job additions even as Q2 GDP growth slowed to a 1.5% annualized pace, a mix of firm hiring and cooling growth that narrows the Fed's room to pause.
  • Stock futures traded slightly lower ahead of today's Q2 GDP revision and the Fed Beige Book, with investors rotating out of rate-sensitive growth names as tightening odds rise.

GGeopolitics

  • Iran's conflict with the US and Israel widened beyond nuclear sites to regional infrastructure, and an Iranian missile targeted a UAE vessel near the Strait of Hormuz, the channel that carries roughly 30% of the world's seaborne oil.
  • The IEA has called the resulting disruption the largest oil supply shock in the history of the global market, a direct threat to energy costs and inflation worldwide.
  • China imposed an 84% retaliatory tariff on US imports as US tariffs on China reached 104%, and the US added new 10-12.5% tariffs on dozens of other trading partners, raising costs across global supply chains.
  • Canada and Mexico tariffs remain stuck at 25% (10% on energy) as a renewal deal stalls, adding roughly $900 a year to the average US household's costs.

TTech and AI

  • The FTC widened its Microsoft antitrust probe to cloud bundling and Copilot AI, and the EU opened a formal investigation into whether Google used publisher and YouTube content without compensation to train its AI models.
  • California's AI Transparency Act took effect August 2, becoming the first major US state law directly regulating AI systems, a template other states are likely to follow.
  • Nvidia and OpenAI's $100 billion compute partnership and Amazon's Anthropic stake, which alone added $16.8 billion to Amazon's Q1 earnings, show AI capital concentrating in a handful of bets that now anchor Big Tech's valuations.

EEnergy and Commodities

  • Crude extended its climb, with WTI at $77.08 and Brent at $82.21, up more than 22% year over year, as the Strait of Hormuz standoff and tightening LNG markets push fuel toward record highs.
  • China's crude imports jumped 22% to 8.45 million barrels a day, anchoring global demand even as supply risk builds from the Middle East.
  • US natural gas held near $2.77/MMBtu despite a smaller than expected storage build, and since gas sets the marginal price in most power markets, that tightness is already feeding into higher wholesale electricity prices.

WWildcard

  • A total solar eclipse crosses Greenland, Iceland, Portugal, and northern Spain on August 12, briefly disrupting transatlantic flight paths and satellite-linked communications.
  • Spain's Ceuta enclave is seeing a sudden surge of migrants from Morocco, adding strain to southern Europe's labor markets and social infrastructure just as the EU navigates tariff and energy pressure elsewhere.
