MMarkets and Economy

  • The Fed raised rates to 3.75%-4.00%, its first hike in over three years, and FOMC signaled at least one more hike in 2026 with markets pricing three additional hikes by mid-2027; this extends a tightening cycle that raises borrowing costs and pressures risk assets.
