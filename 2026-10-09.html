MMarkets and Economy

GGeopolitics

  • US-China tariffs have escalated to 35.2%, and the average US tariff rate has surged to 16.3% from 3% at the start of the year, raising the odds of renewed consumer inflation on imported goods.
  • A 50% US tariff on Canadian goods and a 25% tariff on Brazil threaten auto, energy, agriculture, and food supply chains that are tightly integrated with the US.
  • Fighting tied to the Iran conflict has put pressure on the Strait of Hormuz, a corridor for roughly a quarter of global oil trade, adding a geopolitical risk premium to energy prices even as a ceasefire is negotiated.

TTech and AI

  • OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and xAI face an antitrust suit alleging the labs coordinated to slow AI development, a claim that cuts against the narrative of unchecked AI competition.
  • The UK's competition regulator is moving to force Google to offer rival AI assistants on Android, pressuring its search and AI distribution advantage; separately, a federal judge rejected a DOJ push to force a sale of Google's ad exchange.

EEnergy and Commodities

  • Brent crude jumped to $108 a barrel after a string of tanker attacks in the Strait of Hormuz, a chokepoint for roughly a fifth of global oil supply, before easing on word the US won't strike Iran before the election.

  • Natural gas has fallen to roughly $3.13/MMBtu, with winter forwards running about 24% below last year's levels, pointing to a milder heating season for US households and businesses.
