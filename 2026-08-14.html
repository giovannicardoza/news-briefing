MMarkets and Economy

GGeopolitics

  • A June ceasefire meant to reopen the Strait of Hormuz is fraying: the EIA reports renewed disruption to crude transits and stalled diplomacy, keeping about 30% of global seaborne oil at risk.

  • AI pricing is diverging: OpenAI and Anthropic are cutting prices while DeepSeek raises them, and Apple is training a China only model with Alibaba, signaling deeper regional AI fragmentation.
