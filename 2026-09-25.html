GGeopolitics

  • Congress passed the Graham Act authorizing tariffs up to 100% on Iran and Russia, hardening the US posture toward both economies and raising the odds of retaliatory measures.
  • The US imposed a 50% tariff on Canadian goods and Canada retaliated in kind, escalating a trade dispute between two deeply integrated economies.
  • New Section 232 tariffs of 100% on pharmaceutical imports take effect September 29, raising costs for active ingredients the US sources from targeted countries.
