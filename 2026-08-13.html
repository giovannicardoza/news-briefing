GGeopolitics

TTech and AI

  • The EU AI Act is now fully enforced, requiring high-risk AI systems to meet transparency and deployer obligations across critical infrastructure and employment, raising compliance costs for firms operating in Europe.
  • The FTC is probing Microsoft over bundling Copilot, the first major US antitrust case treating generative AI as a potential illegal bundling practice rather than a standalone feature.
  • Amazon and Alphabet posted massive AI related paper gains this earnings season: Amazon's Anthropic stake added $16.8 billion and Alphabet reported $28.7 billion, underscoring how central AI bets have become to Big Tech valuations.
  • US federal AI regulation remains stalled, leaving states like Colorado, California, and New York to build a patchwork of rules that complicates compliance for multistate tech firms.
