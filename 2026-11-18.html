GGeopolitics

TTech and AI

  • A multi-company antitrust coalition sued Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI, and SpaceX over unfair competition practices, while California separately sued OpenAI over AI agent safeguards: regulatory pressure on the leading AI labs is escalating on multiple fronts.

  • The Federal Reserve flagged AI as a top financial-stability risk, with 87% of its business contacts citing AI-related concerns, a signal that the AI investment boom is itself becoming a source of systemic risk.
